01 · S1 · entry, modelled on the reference screenshot

Ask Photos home

  • Same structure as the reference: back arrow, people and pets row, recents, suggested questions, and the Search or ask bar.
  • Can't remember the photo clearly sits top right as a small outlined chip, deliberately quiet (flow step 2).
  • Tapping it switches the bar's placeholder to “Describe what you remember”; the chip shows as selected (✕ to leave). The user then types a query in the same bar. There is no separate query screen, and autocomplete is out of scope.
  • Faces are sample portraits; the last avatar is a pet (animals count as people).

Understanding the query
